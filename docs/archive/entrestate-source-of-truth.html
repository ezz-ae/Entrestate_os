Part I
Product Architecture
Three domains, one mobile app, one intelligence engine. All knowledge is free. Revenue comes from professional tools.

Entrestate is not an app — it is the underlying infrastructure that will power the next decade of property decisions. A Demand-Driven Intelligence OS that moves UAE real estate from static oracles to predictive infrastructure. The paradigm shift: knowledge is no longer a product — it is the distribution engine; apps are the revenue engine. The AI is not the brain; it is merely the narrator. The mathematical core executes pure SQL logic against the Neon Postgres spine. Zero hallucinations. Every number is verifiable data.

All knowledge, AI chat, project profiles, area intelligence, developer scores, and research reports are completely free and publicly accessible — optimized for SEO. Monetization happens through the Action Board — contextual data tools embedded where the data lives, each with its own trial and tier-gated access. The hourglass model: Intake (free knowledge drives organic SEO) → Conversion (Action Board tools monetize individual professionals) → Expansion (Agent Deployments act as lead-gen for enterprises). The more free users we have, the smarter the predictive demand engine becomes for paying enterprises.

Capital Flow Engine — The Signal Transition

The platform is transitioning from legacy deterministic BUY/HOLD/WAIT labels to a real-time Predictive Demand Level signal driven by K-Means clustering and capital flow monitoring — the Capital Flow Engine. Legacy signals reflect what has happened — static, easily replicated, quickly outdated. The Capital Flow Engine captures what is happening: real-time DLD transaction volume (AED 4.2B+ daily), investor intent from the Agentic Sensor Network, and absorption velocity. The system identifies "Dead Inventory" and "Oversupply Risk" by monitoring the delta between listing counts and DLD transaction velocity.

The Decision Tunnel operationalizes this through 4 epistemic stages: Intent (parameter extraction from NL) → Evidence (data hygiene, exclusion policies, source verification) → Judgment (match scoring, investor profiling) → Action (branded decision objects: PDFs, term sheets, cards).

Value-Moment Registration — No Forms, No Friction

Standard /signup and /login routes are deprecated. Registration happens as a natural side effect of receiving value — not as a gate before it.

Trigger: Value Delivery
After 3–5 substantive exchanges indicating high intent, the agent offers to send the user a report or save their chat session. The user gives their phone/email to receive the deliverable — and registration happens as part of that value delivery. The registration is the value moment. No OTP screens. No signup pages. No friction.
Same Architecture, Different Prompt
The Behavioral Lead Agent uses the same chat infrastructure and tool system. Only the prompt changes — instead of asking "enter your phone to continue," it says "I've prepared a full analysis — where should I send it?" The registration is invisible. The value is visible.
Investor Archetyping
During the exchange, the agent infers Investor Archetypes (Conservative, Yield-Focused, Golden Visa, Growth-Oriented). Profiles stored in Neon to adjust Match Scores and filter inventory to the user's inferred risk tolerance and liquidity needs. By the time the user registers, the platform already knows who they are.
Decision Objects, Not Dashboards

Professionals don't want more dashboards to interpret — they want outcomes they can deploy. The OS translates Demand Level metrics directly into branded, actionable artifacts. Turn an hour of research into a deployable asset in 30 seconds.

Scoring Engines

Market Score v1 — Investment Signal
Composite Score
0–100 investment signal. Weights: price position (vs cohort), yield profile, developer reliability, temporal momentum, confidence grade. Output: BUY / HOLD / WAIT + stress grades A–D.
Match Score
Per-investor fit. Risk tolerance × horizon × liquidity preference × yield vs safety bias. Configurable weights. Builds investor-specific rankings from the same underlying data.

Part VI
AI Architecture — The Decision Tunnel
From raw signal to investment verdict. Four phases: Agentic Sensor Network → Tiered Intelligence Stack → Audit Trail & Judgment → Actionable Verdict.

The Decision Tunnel is the core architectural concept: every investor interaction — whether it arrives via Instagram DM, WhatsApp, a broker's website widget, or direct chat — enters a single funnel that transforms raw demand signals into auditable, actionable investment verdicts. The tunnel has four phases, each with a distinct contract.

Phase 1 — The Agentic Sensor Network

Specialized AI agents deployed on broker websites, Instagram DMs, and WhatsApp act as "sensors" — capturing real-time investor intent and market queries across channels. Data flows from Website Chat widgets, Instagram DM automation, and WhatsApp Business integrations into the unified Intelligence Router. Every query is a demand signal.

Instagram DM Agent
Auto-respond to DMs with market intelligence. Lead qualification. Hot-lead flagging. Captures investor intent from social engagement.
WhatsApp Business Agent
Native AI with project cards + interactive buttons. Captures queries from the most-used channel in the Gulf market. Twilio integration.
Website Chat Widgets
Embedded on broker/developer websites. Brand-customized, scope-controlled. Captures demand signals from visitors already browsing properties.
Broker Website Integrations
Full website integration via Website Updater app. Keeps broker sites in sync with live market data. Every page view is a demand signal.
Multi-Channel Ingestion
All channels feed into one unified Intelligence Router. No channel is siloed. Every signal — regardless of source — enters the same Decision Tunnel and produces the same quality of intelligence.
Network Intelligence
Anonymized, aggregated demand data from all agents across all clients flows into /admin dashboard: trending projects, areas, questions. This is the global demand signal that no single broker can see alone.
Phase 2 — The Tiered Intelligence Stack

The intelligence stack has two distinct layers with a hard boundary between them. Layer 1 uses AI. Layer 2 does not. This separation is the architectural guarantee against hallucination.

Layer 1 — Intent Extraction (AI Layer)
What Happens Here
High-performance LLMs translate Natural Language into a structured TableSpec — a machine-readable query object containing project, area, and budget parameters. This is the only place AI makes decisions about what data to fetch.
Ask Compiler Contract
LLM produces only: TableSpec JSON + Narrative Draft + Citations mapping keys. If LLM fails to produce valid TableSpec → falls back to Golden Paths suggestions. No raw LLM SQL. Ever.
TableSpec Schema (Zod)
scope: geo filters (city/area/project), time grain, signals (columns from Column Registry), exclusions, joins, sorting. Validated at runtime. Invalid specs rejected before any database call.
The Tiered Intelligence Stack — 3 Provider Tiers

A provider-agnostic router optimizing for speed, cost, and depth. Queries are routed through three tiers based on complexity. The LLM never generates raw SQL — it produces the TableSpec, which the backend converts into sanitized prisma.$queryRaw calls against the Neon spine.

Layer 2 — Deterministic Execution (The Engine Room)
The Critical Rule
No AI is used in this layer. This is the "Engine Room" where pure code and SQL run against the Neon Postgres "Spine" to fetch 100% accurate, hallucination-free data. The TableSpec from Layer 1 is translated into deterministic queries by a code-only query builder.
Query Builder
Deterministic query builder from TableSpec. No LLM-generated SQL. The builder maps TableSpec fields to Column Registry entries, validates tier access, applies exclusion policies, and constructs sanitized prisma.$queryRaw calls against the Neon spine. Parameterized queries only.
Neon Postgres "Spine"
inventory: 7,015 × 155 columns. The single source of truth. All queries ultimately resolve against this spine. price_aed as DOUBLE PRECISION end-to-end.
Hallucination Guarantee
Because Layer 2 is deterministic (no AI), every number in the output is traceable to a specific database row and column. This is what makes the Evidence Drawer possible — every claim has a verifiable source.
Phase 3 — The Audit Trail & Judgment
Evidence Drawer
Every decision includes an "Evidence Drawer" that reveals the specific data sources, exclusion policies (e.g., removing distressed sales), and assumptions used. Always shows: sources/views used, exclusion policy applied, assumptions, calculation steps. Includes requestId always.
why.entrestate.com — Audit Trail
Dedicated verification surface. Any AI output that claims a number must be auditable. Users can search for any claim and see the exact data behind it.
The Verification Link
Users can drill down from a narrative response directly into the raw data rows to "Verify the Math" behind any score. Citation click highlights exact rows/columns used in Time Table. Split-screen: narrative left, data table right.
"Verify the Math" Trust Model
Enterprise users can drill from narrative → evidence → raw data. This is not a feature — it is the trust architecture. No black-box scores. Every number has a paper trail.
Phase 4 — The Actionable Verdict
Final Demand Level Verdict
Every project resolves to a clear investment signal: BUY / HOLD / WAIT. Each accompanied by a Stress Grade (A–D) indicating data confidence and risk level. This is the terminal output of the Decision Tunnel.
Generation of Decision Objects
The final verdict is packaged into actionable formats for immediate professional use: branded PDF memos (via fpdf2), investment reports (full analysis documents), and shareable web cards (embeddable verdict cards with deep links).
Delivery Channels
Decision Objects delivered via: PDF download, email (SendGrid), WhatsApp (Twilio), in-app notification, shareable URL with deep link back to full intelligence page.

Soft-Gating Strategy — Gate the Verdict, Not the Data
What's Free
The full project card: name, developer, area, price range, dates, basic metrics. All SEO-indexed. All publicly accessible. The data itself is never paywalled.
What's Blurred
The specific BUY/HOLD/WAIT signal and Developer Reliability Score. Show the card but blur the verdict. This creates the "Anxiety Gap" — the user sees enough to know the answer exists but needs to register/pay to see it. The verdict is the product.
What's Gated by Registration
Persistence. Saved context, watchlists, chat history, workspace, activated tools. The data is free. The ability to build on it over time requires registration. Gate the persistence, not the data.
